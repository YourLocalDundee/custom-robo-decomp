atlas_legion_arena: Atlas (robo 0) with new motions, and Legion Arena (stage 0, Basic Arena's slot) - one ISO to test
"new everything".

ISO:   build/mods/atlas_legion_arena/atlas_legion_arena.iso      patch: mods/atlas_legion_arena/atlas_legion_arena.crpatch

WHAT IS IN IT
1. Atlas, robo 0 (Ray 01's slot, chara entries 000-004): a Roman general made with AI (make_body.py; "Atlas from
   AI" below), the "Hollywood hero" look: polished steel helmet with gold brow band and a tall crimson crest, bearded
   face, sculpted steel muscle cuirass with a gold eagle, gold-tipped crimson leather strips at shoulders and hips,
   crimson tunic, embossed steel greaves, leather bracers and boots; a curved red scutum (bronze boss, gold
   lightning) rigid on the left forearm and a gladius in the right fist. 3,148 triangles (--tris=3150: body 2,650,
   scutum 300, gladius 198) on Ray 01's own skeleton, one 512x256 texture baked from the AI models' 2K colour maps
   with their ambient occlusion, metal graded to iron, specular mask from the metallic maps. 234,499 bytes per entry
   (limit 237,568).
2. Motions (make_motion.py -> files/motion.DBN; make_mod.py packs it into logo_read_us.dbn entry 8):
   - shooting: the 6 sf_weapon_* motions get the shield brace (off hand across the chest, chest leaned 6 degrees into
     the shot); the two recoil motions (sf_weapon_stiff, sf_weapon_jump_stiff) get the cadence: a triple tap
     "da-da-DUM" (kicks on frames 0, 6, 12, the last the strongest), the chest rocking back on each beat. The gun
     hand's aim stays the game's. Every sf_ robo shares these motions (P2 too, if it is an sf_ body).
   - air dash (sf_jump_dash): the spear dive - leaned 40 degrees forward, shield thrust ahead, gun arm swept back,
     legs straight and trailing.
   - charge attack (cr_atk_sf011_tackl_n, robo 0's own): the shield bash - wind-up, rush with the shield punched
     ahead at shoulder height, recovery; the stock forward travel kept.
   - victory 1 (sf_win_sf011_01): gladiator salute - standing tall, shield planted, gun raised and pumped 3 times.
   - victory 2 (sf_win_sf011_02): shield raised overhead, gun pointed up, then 3 strikes of the gun on the shield.
   - lose (sf_lose_sf011_01): down on one knee, chest and head slumped, gun arm hanging, shield leaned in front.
   - every other motion of the body (25 more, so all 38 of robo 0's slots are new): the shield held up in front
     while standing (a slow breath), walking and running (leaning 6 / 14 degrees into the run; back-runs 2 / 4;
     legs and the gun-arm swing stock), bomb and pod motions (the gun brace), held overhead on the double jump,
     braced low on both landings, covering the face on all 5 knock-downs (in a frame that tips with the body), low to
     guard while getting up, and a quick raised-shield salute on the transform (sf_change).
     sf_ motions are shared: every robo of the same body type (bodies 1, 2, 10-17, 20, 24, ...) moves the same way.
   Rules kept: no root / joint 0 tracks; every track ends on the motion's last frame. Float pools: group 0 16,147
   of 16,384, group 1 10,775.
3. Charge effect and particles (Atlas's own only; shared shots, hits and leg jets stay stock):
   - attack_effect entry 000 (robo 0's charge): its 3 paletted textures (aura band, impact flash, shockwave ring)
     recoloured from blue to gold (tools/recolour_dat.py, palettes only; models/attack_effect/).
   - the charge's particles: generators 4000, 4001 (fired by piece 0) and 4005, 4006 (spawned by 4002, piece 1),
     used by nothing else, recoloured gold (particles.json; tools/ptcl.py; make_mod.py applies it to
     logo_read_us.dbn entry 0 in place, 10 colour commands, same size).
4. Legion Arena (make_stage.py + tools/blender/legion_arena.py; bg_data entries 000 and 001):
   - a Roman amphitheatre around Basic Arena's 16 x 16 floor: sand, a brick podium wall with marble coping and two
     wooden gates, red banners on bronze rods, 9 tiers of travertine seats rounding into an oval, a marble
     colonnade, a paved terrace, upper seating, a three-storey arcade facade with 16 banners, a sunset sky dome.
   - textures: CC0 photo materials from ambientCG (Ground080 sand, Travertine009, Bricks076C, PavingStones151,
     Marble021, Planks037A, Fabric085; build/legion/cc0, downloaded by make_stage.py), a painted arch texture, a
     bronze texture, a sky; all CMP 256 / 128.
   - lighting baked into vertex colours (sun from the stage light's direction with ray-cast shadows, sky light with
     ambient occlusion); the materials are the stage's own unlit "texture x vertex colour" kind.
   - obstacles on their exact collision boxes: stone walls with marble coping (models 1, 5, 6, 7), wooden
     barricades with bronze bands (models 8, 9).
   - moving: model 9 (the east barricade) slides 2.4 toward the centre and back every 8 seconds (a looping joint
     animation added to the scene) and its collision group has flag 8, so robos are pushed by it.
   - breakable: the three pods (models 2-4, hit points 100 / 200 / 400) kept with their break animations,
     recoloured bronze and gold.
   - lights warm (ambient 120,100,92; sun 255,226,186), fog added (colour 232,168,120 = the background; haze from 60).
   - entry 000: 741,823 bytes (load region 0x100000); collision 001: Basic Arena's, group 9 flag 8.

ATLAS FROM AI (all local, on the RTX 5080; D:\ai)
  1. a pose guide: Atlas's legionnaire body rendered front-on in its T-pose, colour + depth (D:\ai\work\depth_render.py)
  2. FLUX.2-dev (Black Forest Labs, 32B; D:\ai\work\gen_flux2b.py, look "hero"): the guide render turned into a
     photorealistic Roman general in exactly that pose (picked flux2/f_hero_0.png, seed 600), cut out by cutout.py.
     Two passes so it fits 16 GB VRAM + 48 GB RAM: the 24B text encoder (bnb 4-bit) encodes the prompt and exits;
     then the transformer (GGUF Q6_K, unsloth/FLUX.2-dev-GGUF) is held in RAM and group-offloaded block by block to
     the GPU (9.6 s/step, ~6.5 min an image). The earlier body came from Qwen-Image-2.1 (20B, 4-bit nf4, image edit,
     ~1 s/step; gen_qwen21.py, q21/e_0.png). The props: Qwen-Image-2.1 text-to-image, product shots on white
     (gen_props.py; scutum_0, gladius_0); the scutum cut out by cutout.py (Hunyuan's own background remover kept
     only the boss). Tried first: SDXL + depth ControlNet (looked like a mannequin), Qwen-Image 20B +
     ControlNet-Union (noisy), FLUX.2-dev's bnb 4-bit build (overflowed 16 GB VRAM; crashed in cuBLAS when streamed).
  3. Hunyuan3D 2.1 (Tencent, open weights; Windows portable pack v4-cu129 in D:\ai\hy3d): shape and PBR paint (40k
     faces, 2K colour / metallic / roughness; the body painted at 768) (D:\ai\work\hy21.py; outputs in hy_hero,
     hy_scutum_cut, hy_gladius_0)
     Setup it needed: CUDA 12.9, VS 2022 Build Tools (CUDA 12.9 rejects VS 2026's compiler), pip fast_simplification,
     simplify_mesh_utils.py patched (face_count=) for trimesh 4.7.
  4. tools/blender/atlas_ai_body.py: fit onto the guide, decimate, weight to Ray 01's bones, hands / feet onto their
     IK effector joints, props rigid (scutum on the left forearm's mesh joint, gladius on the right hand's, moved with
     the hand to its bind position), bake, export; HSDRawViewer --import --replace over chara 000-004 (make_body.py).
