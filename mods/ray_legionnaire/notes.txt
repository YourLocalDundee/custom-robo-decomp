ray_legionnaire - Ray 01 (robo 0, chara entries 000-004) as a Roman legionnaire. Test edit of the Blender loop
(docs/MODDING.md "Blender loop", plan.md step 70).

What the edit is (tools/blender/legionnaire.py):
- crest: a tall red box (0.30 x model height) on a gold stand, front to back on top of the head, weighted to the
  head role bone JOBJ_32
- scutum: a red 0.30 x 0.50 (model height) shield with a gold rim, spine and boss, in front of the robo's left
  forearm, weighted to arm_b_fore JOBJ_49 (left = up x forward; forward = hips -> toes, the bind pose's feet stand
  toward glTF +Z / Blender -Y)
- bronze body: the 512x512 CMP body texture recoloured to luminance x bronze
- skeleton unchanged (55 joints, same order and names), so no roles file; roles and joint tree checked equal
  after the rebuild

Rebuild (Windows; needs the one-time build, Blender 4.2+ and HSDRawViewer from tools/setup.ps1):
  pwsh tools\model_loop.ps1 -Mod ray_legionnaire -Script legionnaire -Entries 000,001,002,003,004 -Boot
Output (local, never committed: they hold game data):
  mods\ray_legionnaire\models\chara\chara_000.dat .. chara_004.dat
  build\mods\ray_legionnaire\ray_legionnaire.iso
