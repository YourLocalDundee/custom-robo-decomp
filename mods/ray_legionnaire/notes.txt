ray_legionnaire - Ray 01 (robo 0, chara entries 000-004) completely replaced by a Roman legionnaire (plan.md step 73;
step 70's first try was a crest and a plank added to Ray 01's own body).

Built by tools/blender/legionnaire_v2.py in headless Blender: primitives, a solidify modifier, procedural shaders
baked with Cycles (base colour x ambient occlusion) into one image per material; nothing of Ray 01's meshes,
materials or textures is kept. Preview: docs/images/legionnaire_v2_blender.png; the built .dat exported again and
rendered: docs/images/legionnaire_v2_reimport.png.

Model: 118 parts in 7 materials, 3,756 triangles (Ray 01's body: 2,465). Images (CMP, each with a half-size specular mask):
Iron 128, Bronze 128, Gold 64, Red 128, Leather 128, Skin 256 (the face gets 3x the texels), Scutum 128 (front 4x).
Skeleton: Ray 01's 55 joints with their transforms, plus Crest (under the head JOBJ_32), Scutum (under the left
forearm JOBJ_39) and Gladius (under the right hand JOBJ_54): 58 joints; the import writes chara_NNN.roles.json
(arm_a_shoulder 35 -> 36 ... arm_b_hand 51 -> 53) and make_mod.py rebuilds blocks B and C from it; block A (limb
measurements) stays Ray 01's (the IK chain lengths are Ray 01's).
Skinning: skin of arms / legs / neck, tunic core and skirt, pteruges are envelope (smooth) over 13 joints; the
rest is rigid on the joints that carried Ray 01's parts. Hands and feet sit on IK effector chains: they are built
where they belong and moved to where Ray 01's own hand / foot meshes sit in the game data (<edited>.pose.json).
Left / right: Ray 01 faces Blender +Y, so arm_a (-X) is its left: scutum on arm_a, gladius in arm_b's hand
(the v1 photo showed v1's shield, put on arm_b, on the gun arm).

Rebuild (Windows; needs the one-time build, Blender 4.2+ and HSDRawViewer from tools/setup.ps1):
  pwsh tools\model_loop.ps1 -Mod ray_legionnaire -Script legionnaire_v2 -Entries 000,001,002,003,004 -Boot
Output (local, never committed: they hold game data):
  mods\ray_legionnaire\models\chara\chara_000.dat .. chara_004.dat (+ chara_NNN.roles.json)
  build\mods\ray_legionnaire\ray_legionnaire.iso
  mods\ray_legionnaire\ray_legionnaire.crpatch
