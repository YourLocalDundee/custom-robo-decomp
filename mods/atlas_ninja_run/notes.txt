What make_motion.py writes:
- Roman march + off-arm flail on the 8 walk / run motions of robo 0's battle motion set (slots 1-6, 8, 9: sf_normal_walk,
  sf_normal_run, sf_normal_walk_weapon_r180, sf_normal_run_weapon_r180, sf_back_walk_weapon_r180, sf_back_run_weapon_r180,
  sf_back_run_guard_r180, sf_normal_run_guard_r180): upright, chest out 20 degrees, each swinging foot kicked forward on
  a straight leg (up to hip height); the off hand (A) windmills beside the shoulder, about 4 turns a second. The gun hand
  is left to the game.
- Hip-thrust air dash on sf_jump_dash (slot 10): chest arched back 50 degrees, both arms thrown back, legs trailing
  straight, the whole pose turned back 20 degrees by its targets (no root or joint 0 edit).
- Butt shot on all fours on the 6 sf_weapon_* motions: turned (root.roty + GUN_TURN, default 160), bent over 150 degrees
  (butt the highest point), legs straight and spread, the gun hand under the body between the legs, the wrist
  (joint14.rotz -60) tipping the barrel up through the butt. Robo 0's arms are too short for the off hand to reach the
  ground.
Every sf_ body shares these motions, so other sf_ robos (P2 included) move the same way.
